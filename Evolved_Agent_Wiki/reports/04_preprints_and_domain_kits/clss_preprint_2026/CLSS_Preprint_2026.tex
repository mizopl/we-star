\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Prevailing approaches to automated mathematical and algorithmic discovery---such as AlphaTensor, FunSearch, AlphaEvolve, and PatternBoost---rely on distributed cloud clusters of GPUs or TPUs to train specialized neural policies or evolve heuristics across hundreds of thousands of evaluations. In this paper, we report the discovery and machine verification of \textbf{over 80 new world records across eight distinct scientific domains}, computed entirely locally over a single weekend on a standard office computer (Apple M1 Pro, 10 CPU cores, 16\,GB RAM, zero cloud compute). Our method, \textbf{CLSS (\emph{Compositional Lifting and Seam Surgery})}, exploits a universal structural artifact in human-constructed mathematical record tables: to verify correctness on paper, human researchers compose smaller exact building blocks using rigid, uniform algebraic rules (Kronecker products, symmetric block splits, group lifts, concentric atomic shells, or unpruned digit bases), leaving localized structural redundancy---a \textbf{compositional seam}---at component boundaries. By concentrating local exact optimization exclusively on these boundary seams, we establish new certified world records in:
(1) \textbf{Operations Research} (31 of 60 official IHTC-2024 hospital timetabling instances, led by \texttt{i27}: $50{,}976 \to \mathbf{50{,}063}$),
(2) \textbf{Combinatorial Design Theory} (20 covering design records, led by $C(94,13,5) \le \mathbf{125{,}184}$, $-4{,}019$ blocks),
(3) \textbf{Bioinformatics} (17 DNA minimizer density records, led by $(w{=}12,k{=}4)$ at $\mathbf{0.1336292920}$, $-1.776\%$),
(4) \textbf{Nanomaterials Physics} (8 putative global minima for 55-atom $\text{Cu}_n\text{Ag}_{55-n}$ Gupta nanoalloys in the Cambridge Cluster Database, led by $\text{Cu}_{39}\text{Ag}_{16}$ with $\Delta E = \mathbf{-1.18984440\text{ eV}}$),
(5) \textbf{Symbolic Algorithms} (4 ternary fast matrix multiplication records in $\mathbb{Z}_T=\{-1,0,1\}$, led by $\langle 14,15,15\rangle$ at rank $\mathbf{1{,}894}$ and $\langle 9,12,14\rangle$ at rank $\mathbf{954}$),
(6) \textbf{Extremal Graph Theory} (a degree--diameter $(14,3)$ graph on $\mathbf{N=1{,}118}$ vertices),
(7) \textbf{Statistical Mechanics} (exact 44-digit $\text{A054759}(15)$ and the complete 80-entry antidiagonal $d=79$ of $\text{A089980}$ up to 279 digits), and
(8) \textbf{Additive Number Theory \& Erd\H{o}s Problems} (an explicit finite-depth $N=10^{13}$ lower bound $C_{3a}(V) > \mathbf{1.19230254}$ for the Gyarmati--Hennecart--Ruzsa sum-difference constant, beating the finite-certified record $1.19102809$ by $+0.00127445$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction: The Seam Principle \& Local Office-Computer Discovery}

Across discrete mathematics, additive number theory, operations research, bioinformatics, and computational physics, public benchmark repositories track the best known explicit constructions and bounds. In recent years, beating entries in these tables has been widely assumed to require industrial-scale compute clusters.

We demonstrate that industrial cloud compute is unnecessary when search is guided by structural seam analysis. Because our entire cross-disciplinary campaign was executed locally over a single Sunday on a standard office computer without external accelerators, the acronym \textbf{CLSS} equally stands for \textbf{\emph{COMPUTER LAZY SUNDAY SOLVER}}. Why is local execution on a standard office machine scientifically significant?
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep=1pt
    \item \textbf{Mathematical Structure Beats Brute-Force Scale:} At medium-to-large scales ($v=94$ covering designs with $5.49 \times 10^7$ quintuples, $14 \times 15 \times 15$ matrix tensors with $9.92 \times 10^6$ equations, or $N=10^{13}$ digit sets), unconstrained search spaces exceed $10^{1000}$ states. No GPU cluster can brute-force them; however, isolating the low-dimensional \emph{compositional seam} between known building blocks reduces the active degrees of freedom to a tractable subproblem solvable in minutes on a 10-core CPU.
    \item \textbf{Zero-Barrier Reproducibility:} Every certificate reported in this work can be independently verified from scratch in under 5 seconds on any standard desktop or office laptop.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Domain-Specific Results and Mathematical Certificates}

\subsection{Domain 1: Healthcare Operations Research --- 31 New World Records on IHTC-2024}
The Integrated Healthcare Timetabling Competition 2024 (IHTC-2024) couples surgical theater scheduling, patient admission, room assignment, and nurse shift assignment across 84 shifts. Single-shift nurse swaps cannot cross the \texttt{ContinuityOfCare} barrier when a nurse covers $k \ge 2$ shifts of a patient's stay. Introducing \textbf{Compound Patient-Stay Nurse Consolidation} (replacing a dropped nurse $n_{\text{drop}} \to n_{\text{keep}}$ simultaneously across all shifts of a patient's stay in $O(1)$ incremental time) followed by exact HiGHS MILP polishing over 6-nurse subsets improved **31 out of 60 official competition instances** (`i01--i30`, `m01--m30`), led by public benchmark \texttt{i27} ($50{,}976 \to \mathbf{50{,}063}$, $\mathbf{-913}$ soft cost, $0$ hard violations, PR~\#416) and hidden instance \texttt{m28} ($45{,}913 \to \mathbf{45{,}873}$, $\mathbf{-40}$, PR~\#431), alongside \texttt{m23} ($-434$), \texttt{m16} ($-287$), \texttt{i21} ($-271$), and \texttt{m27} ($-243$).

\subsection{Domain 3: Additive Number Theory \& Erd\H{o}s Sum-Difference Constant $C_{3a} > 1.19230254$}
In the tradition of Erd\H{o}s and Ruzsa on sum-difference asymmetry, the Gyarmati--Hennecart--Ruzsa constant $C_{3a}$ is the supremum of exponents $\theta$ for which arbitrarily large finite sets satisfy $|A-B| \gg |A+B|^\theta$. By the GHR Lemma (tracked on Terence Tao's \texttt{teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/main/constants/3a.md}), any finite set $V \subset \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ with $0 \in V$ and $|V-V| < 2\max V + 1$ certifies $C_{3a} \ge 1 + \frac{\log(|V-V|/|V+V|)}{\log(2\max V + 1)}$.

Prior work by Kleinwaks (2026) achieved an asymptotic limit bound of $1.19519192^*$ as $N \to \infty$ in even base $q = 27{,}022$, but its explicit finite-depth certificate at $N = 10^{13}$ had to fall back to the large uncontrolled-carry base $Q = 2B + 1 = 34{,}065$ ($B = 17{,}032$), capping the published finite-certified record at $\mathbf{1.19102809}$. We observed that the formal injectivity theorem (\texttt{residue\_word\_injective} in \texttt{CoreConstruction.lean}) requires only that the pruned difference alphabet $\widetilde{D} \subseteq M - M$ have pairwise distinct residues modulo an \emph{odd} base $q < 2B + 1$. Partitioning non-zero residues modulo odd $q = 27{,}995$ into $13{,}997$ complementary pairs $\{r, q-r\}$ and selecting the minimum digit-sum representative $\kappa(d)$ on mask $M = \operatorname{span}_{\mathbb{N}_0}(\{3,4\}\times\{506,508,529\}) \cap [0, 17086]$ ($|M|=3{,}153, |S|=18{,}835$) yields a **complete residue system modulo $27{,}995$** ($|\widetilde{D}_+| = 13{,}997$, $|\widetilde{D}| = 27{,}995 = q$). Setting $V = 2U$ at finite depth $N = 10^{13}$ ($n_0 = 17{,}074{,}745{,}018$, $L = 50{,}031{,}092{,}045{,}455{,}301$) certifies:
$$C_{3a}(V) > \mathbf{1.19230254070568368329} \quad (\text{60-dps \texttt{mpmath.iv}}) \quad \text{and} \quad > \mathbf{1.19230254068351294895} \quad (\text{integer Lean Stirling bounds}),$$
strictly beating the published finite-certified record $1.19102809$ by $\mathbf{+0.00127445}$ ($12{,}744\times$ the $10^{-7}$ threshold, V3R1T4S PR~\#485; submitted to Terence Tao's \texttt{teorth/optimizationproblems} as PR~\#236). Even on Kleinwaks's exact mask $B = 17{,}032$, our odd-base pruning at $q = 27{,}995$ certifies $C_{3a}(V) > \mathbf{1.1922990484}$.
