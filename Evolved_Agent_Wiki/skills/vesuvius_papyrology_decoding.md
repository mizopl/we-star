## 2. Dead-End Guard `X13` — Rejected Papyrology Methods (NEVER REPEAT)

1. **Boxed Lupa Cropping + Template/Viterbi Decoding (`Stages M–O`, `X13`):**
   - Cropping rectangular boxes around local fiber intersections, boosting contrast inside the box, and running a template/Viterbi Greek language prior (`greedy_match_frac == 0.0`) hallucinates Greek syllables out of orthogonal `perfibrale`/`transfibrale` reed-fiber crossings.
   - **Mandatory Rule (`X13`):** Never crop or box individual candidate letters on a smoothed background, and never use a language-model Viterbi prior when `greedy_match_frac == 0.0`. Every candidate region MUST first be readable by the naked eye on an **unboxed, continuous full-field `Tracked Z-DoG` + `CT Epidermal Relief` 8-panel sheet** with `Tracked Z-DoG p99 >= 105` and continuous multi-letter stroke ducts.
2. **2D Poisson / Curl-Free Post-Warping (`Stage G`):**
   - Smears and stretches letter edges in 2D instead of tracking the physical 3D sheet normal. Always use 3D `m7-Zarr` Normal Snapping (#2).
3. **Single-Depth (`d = 0`) Ink Evaluation Without `d +- 4..6` Wing Subtraction:**
   - Produces 100% false positives from adjacent-winding bleed-through and thick reed-fiber knots. Always require Two-Sided Thin-Sheet `Z-DoG` (#1).
