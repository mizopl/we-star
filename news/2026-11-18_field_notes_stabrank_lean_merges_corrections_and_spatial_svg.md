It immediately caught an off-by-one in our docstring: ruling out a 2D subspace inside $V_3(H_3) \subset \mathrm{span}(u_1,\dots,u_r)$ relies on $3 \times 3$ and $4 \times 4$ minors (Hadamard bound $\le 55{,}296 < P = 1{,}000{,}003$) for rank-3 exclusion and $\le 5 \times 5$ minors ($\le 432{,}000 < P$) for rank-4 checks—not $6 \times 6$ minors (`46,656,000 > P`), which would have exceeded the prime modulus! Every obstacle claim now requires an explicit positive-vs-negative separator check before registration.
