# Note of Fame — v10 "BRAIN Build" (lived ~1 hour)

**Achieved.** Its first resume ran entirely on the new scaffolding. Preflight was green, the run submitted its long computations to the job queue instead of waiting on them, and the ledger refused self-resolution. It also caught its own first bug within minutes: X4, a relative cwd in a job spec. That bug became a preflight check, which is exactly K4's design.

**Missing.** DECIDE was still a single voice. One commander picks the next move, and its own preferences frame the options. BRAIN §3 proposed an index, but an index computed from the commander's own numbers inherits the commander's blind spots.

**Why retired.** The human proposed a decision process modelled on a tug-of-war between brain regions, and then sharpened it: the debate is not A versus B but IMPLEMENT this hypothesis versus SEARCH for a better one. v11 adds that gate (§13), so the alternative is generated by the opposition instead of being chosen by the commander.
