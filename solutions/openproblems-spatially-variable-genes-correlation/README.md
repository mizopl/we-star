## In Plain Language

Spatial transcriptomics measures gene activity together with `(x, y)` coordinates across thousands of cells or capture spots in a tissue slice, and a foundational task is ranking genes by how coherently their expression forms spatial patterns rather than spatially random shot noise. Existing detectors split into two incompatible camps: global library-size-normalized methods (`MoranI`, `SpatialDE2`, `nnSVG`, `SPARK`, `SpaGFT`) that divide each spot's counts by total spot counts $L_s = \sum_g Y_{s,g}$ — accurate on uniform grids when $L_s$ reflects technical depth, but degraded on irregular single-nucleus `slide-tags`, `MERFISH`, `Stereo-seq`, and compartmented solid tumors where $L_s$ carries biological compartment structure — versus unnormalized projection tests (`SPARK-X`), which avoid $1 / L_s$ contamination on clean simulated counts but lose sensitivity to localized domains, tie at IEEE-754 float64 `p < 1e-300`, and degrade when wet-lab spots suffer technical capture dropout.

[`code/method.py`](./code/method.py) (`score_svg(counts, coords)`) scores each gene column via dual variance-stabilizing transforms (`log1p(y)` and Freeman-Tukey `sqrt(y) + sqrt(y + 1) - 1`) across three tie-free locally density-adapted biweight kNN operators and three `O(2)`-invariant low-rank Mercer polynomial-Gaussian operators, standardized on the unbounded real line by exact Cliff & Ord (1981) kurtosis-adjusted analytical permutation moments. Companion module [`code/ams_svg_eb_v2.py`](./code/ams_svg_eb_v2.py) adds Hilbert-sphere geodesic arc-length scale quadrature (`arccos(R_{s,s+1})`) and a scale-separated Wiener + deflated-spectral Empirical-Bayes high-pass capture gate that removes sub-compartment technical spot-capture dropout while preserving smooth tissue compartments. Running [`verify.py`](./verify.py) executes blind column-permuted recomputation across a 12-dataset, 9-technology panel plus full row-by-row semantic checks across all five JSON certificates in ~25 seconds on CPU with zero bytecode side effects.

Two negative results encountered during Wave 2 formulation further clarify the operator geometry:
- **Kac–Murdock–Szegő Boundary Trap of $H_0$-Whitening (`No-Go #1`):** Across an ordered multi-scale bandwidth ladder, the exact centered null correlation matrix $R_{ab} = \operatorname{tr}(\tilde{W}^{(a)}\tilde{W}^{(b)}) / (\|\tilde{W}^{(a)}\|_F \|\tilde{W}^{(b)}\|_F)$ decays exponentially away from the diagonal. Inverting $R$ (`GLS` $R^{-1}\mathbf{1}$ or Bartholomew–Kudo positive-cone projection $\max_{\boldsymbol{\mu}\ge 0} \mathbf{Z}^T R^{-1} \mathbf{Z}$) concentrates `~78%` of weight onto the two extreme boundary scales (`knn_0` and `mercer_0.50`) while zeroing interior scales, reducing `42_melanoma` from `0.797905` to `0.780190`. Integrating along the Hilbert-sphere geodesic via trapezoid arc-length weights $\Delta\theta_{s,s+1} = \arccos(R_{s,s+1})$ avoids matrix inversion, satisfies exact duplicate-scale invariance (`< 1e-14`), and lifts all `4 / 4` Dev datasets (`0.861095 -> 0.863571`).
- **Spatial-Mixture Variance-Dip Curvature Shift (`No-Go #2`):** Under `scDesign3`'s convex mean law $\boldsymbol{\mu}_{g,\alpha} = \alpha\boldsymbol{\mu}_g + (1-\alpha)\boldsymbol{\mu}_g \circ \pi$, spatial mean variance follows $(1 - 2\alpha(1-\alpha))\operatorname{Var}_s(\mu_g)$, dipping by `50%` at $\alpha = 0.50$. Estimating Negative Binomial dispersion $\hat{\theta}_g$ separately from each column's marginal variance $s_g^2$ therefore varies the nonlinear Anscombe curve across $\alpha$ within the same gene (`0.797905 -> 0.776000` on `42_melanoma`), whereas fixed-curve `log1p` + Freeman-Tukey transforms preserve $\alpha$-comparability.
